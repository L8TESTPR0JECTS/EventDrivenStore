# Cross-Service Data Boundaries (As Implemented)

This document describes **how data boundaries are enforced across services today** in the EventDrivenStore system.

The system is intentionally **decoupled**. Each service owns its data, publishes domain events, and reacts to events from others. **No service directly reads or writes another service’s database.**

---

## Core Principles

- **Single owner per data set**: Each service is the system of record for its own domain.
- **No cross-service DB access**: No direct queries, joins, or writes across service databases.
- **Event-driven integration**: Cross-service data flow happens only through immutable domain events.
- **Local projections are allowed**: Services may store snapshots of external facts needed for their own decisions.
- **At-least-once delivery**: Consumers must be idempotent; duplication is expected and handled.
- **No distributed transactions**: Cross-service consistency is eventual and event-driven.

---

## Service Ownership Summary

| Service | Owns | Consumes | Emits |
|-----|-------|----------|-------|
| Orders | Order intent, item composition, pricing snapshot, order lifecycle state | none (as implemented) | `OrderCreated` |
| Inventory | Stock availability, reservation attempts and outcomes | `OrderCreated` | `InventoryReservationSucceeded`, `InventoryReservationFailed` |
| Billing | Payment attempts and outcomes, pricing snapshots for billing decisions | `OrderCreated`, `InventoryReservationSucceeded` | `PaymentSucceeded`, `PaymentFailed` |

---

## Orders Service Boundary

**Owns**
- Orders and their lifecycle state
- Order item composition and pricing snapshot

**Emits**
- `OrderCreated` when an order is accepted

**Does not**
- Reserve inventory
- Process payments
- Query downstream databases

---

## Inventory Service Boundary

**Owns**
- Product stock levels (`StockItems`)
- Reservation attempts and outcomes (`StockReservations`)
- Consumer idempotency log (`ProcessedEvents`)
- Outbox events (`OutboxEvents`)

**Consumes**
- `OrderCreated`

**Emits**
- `InventoryReservationSucceeded`
- `InventoryReservationFailed`

**Does not**
- Create or mutate orders
- Perform payments
- Depend on orders or billing databases

---

## Billing Service Boundary

**Owns**
- Payment attempts and outcomes (`payments`)
- Pricing snapshots from Orders (`order_pricing_snapshot`)
- Consumer idempotency log (`processed_events`)
- Outbox events (`outbox_events`)

**Consumes**
- `OrderCreated`
- `InventoryReservationSucceeded`

**Emits**
- `PaymentSucceeded`
- `PaymentFailed`

**Does not**
- Mutate orders
- Reserve inventory
- Query orders or inventory databases

---

## Approved Cross-Service Data Patterns

1. **Immutable domain events**
- Each service publishes facts about its own state transitions.
- Downstream services react asynchronously.

2. **Local read models / snapshots**
- Billing stores `order_pricing_snapshot` based on `OrderCreated` so it never queries Orders.

3. **Transactional outbox**
- Inventory and Billing store outgoing events in local `outbox_*` tables and publish via background workers.

4. **Idempotent consumers**
- `ProcessedEvents` / `processed_events` tables prevent duplicate handling on re-delivery.

---

## Forbidden Cross-Service Patterns

- Reading another service’s database directly
- Writing into another service’s database
- Cross-database joins
- Synchronous RPC for authoritative data reads
- Distributed transactions or 2PC

---

## Event Contracts and Compatibility

- Events are the **only integration contract** between services.
- Producers must treat events as public, stable APIs.
- Consumers must tolerate new fields and ignore unknown fields.
- Changes must be **backward compatible** and additive.

---

## Summary

Cross-service data boundaries in EventDrivenStore are enforced through **clear ownership, event-driven integration, and local projections**. The system intentionally trades synchronous coupling for asynchronous consistency, keeping services autonomous, resilient, and independently evolvable.
